Good questions

Before you ask

Straight answers to what buyers ask us most. Still wondering about something? Call us at 480.660.2518.

What's the catch?

No catch. Your credit shows up as a line item on your official Loan Estimate, so you'll see exactly what you're getting in writing in advance and then count all the savings on closing day.

Can I use the credit toward my down payment?

No. Your down payment has to come from you, not the lender. The credit goes toward eligible closing costs and prepaid items, like lender and title fees or prepaid taxes and insurance. That lowers the cash you need at closing, so more of your savings can go toward your down payment or stay in your pocket.

I'm pre-approved with another lender. Is it too late to switch to Moxie?

No. You can switch to Moxie any time before you close. The earlier you switch, the better, so get pre-approved with Moxie today.
